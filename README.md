# Orbit test builds

Orbit puts your day in the MacBook notch: music, meetings, tasks, focus, files, AI agents and
35 add-ons, with Orbit the little planet keeping you company.

These are **test builds**. Download the newest DMG from [Releases](../../releases).

## Install

1. Open the DMG and drag **Orbit** to **Applications**.
2. Open Orbit. macOS says it can't check it for malware, because test builds aren't notarized by Apple yet.
3. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Hover the notch at the top of your screen. Settings are in the menu bar icon or the gear in the notch.

After that, Orbit checks for updates once a day and installs them itself
(or use **Check for updates…** in the menu bar icon).

Needs macOS 14 or later. Works with or without a notch.
